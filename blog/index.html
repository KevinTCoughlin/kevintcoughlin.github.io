    I shipped 11,105 icons to use 17

    Shoutkit's phone app draws 31 Material icons, and the watch app draws three: PlayArrow, Pause, and Stop. Fourteen of the phone's icons come from material-icons-core. The other seventeen came from material-icons-extended. The 1.7.8 Android archive of that library is 11,105 icon classes. Release builds shrink the unused ones away. Debug builds dex every class, and that step is where CI ran out of heap. Copying the seventeen and dropping the dependency took the FOSS debug APK from 29,397,070 bytes to 22,728,773.

    http.Server.Shutdown doesn't cancel request contexts

    In lazydeck, I gave serve one 30 second deadline for the HTTP drain and for reaping in-flight jobs. http.Server.Shutdown waits until handlers return. It leaves their request contexts alone. An open GET /v1/jobs/{id}/events stream sat in that wait long enough to expire the deadline, so the job reap cancelled the job and returned without waiting. execute was still blocked in cmd.Run. The uv, python, ssh, and rsync children are in their own process group, so they kept running after the process exited.
