    I stopped dexing every Material icon in the ShoutKit debug build

    ShoutKit depended on material-icons-extended for a handful of icons. The phone app uses 31 and the watch app 3. Release builds shrink the rest away. Debug builds dex the whole library, and that dex is where CI ran out of heap.
