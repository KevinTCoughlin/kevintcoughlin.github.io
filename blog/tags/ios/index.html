    Listen Now crashed when two recents shared an id

    In ShoutKit, Listen Now keyed Recently Played with Dictionary(uniqueKeysWithValues:). That initializer traps when two keys match. stationID stopped being unique in schema V2, because CloudKit does not support unique attributes, and with iCloud sync on the same station can briefly have two Recently Played rows. Drawing Listen Now in that window crashed. The change is shoutkit#288 (fa434a1), for shoutkit#282.

    Why my radio app dipped to 1/14 volume on every network blip

    In ShoutKit I fade player.volume in from silence when a station rejoins, so the live edge does not arrive as a click. A buffer underrun reports .playing again, and the rejoin fade treated that as a fresh start. The stream came back at full volume, the fade waited one step, then set the volume to 1/14 and swelled back to full. shoutkit#274 runs the fade only after the player was silenced.
