    Don't parse show notes until something reads them

    Podr turned every episode and podcast description that contained < or & into plain text with DOMParser on the main thread, during feed load, before the episode list could update. Ordinary notes are a string scan now. The plain text is computed the first time something reads description, and cached. Laziness beat a worker: the scan needs no DOM, copying a multi-megabyte feed across threads costs more than the scan, and the fallback needs DOMParser, which workers do not have. The change is podr#188 (10c3607). podr#186 left this open. Large descriptions were still parsed in full on the main thread.

    I shipped 11,105 icons to use 17

    ShoutKit's phone app draws 31 Material icons, and the watch app draws three: PlayArrow, Pause, and Stop. Fourteen of the phone's icons come from material-icons-core. The other seventeen came from material-icons-extended. The 1.7.8 Android archive of that library is 11,105 icon classes. Release builds shrink the unused ones away. Debug builds dex every class, and that step is where CI ran out of heap. Copying the seventeen and dropping the dependency took the FOSS debug APK from 29,397,070 bytes to 22,728,773.
